# Candidate data sources — verify before use

This is a shortlist, not blanket permission to download or train. Re-check the exact dataset revision, license file, and local law before use.

| Source | What it may help teach | License / caveat | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [WikiText-2 dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext) | Small English language-modeling baseline | Dataset cards commonly identify CC BY-SA / GFDL for WikiText; confirm the exact revision and obligations. | Conditional; legal review before commercial use |
| [TinyStories dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories) | Simple story structure and next-token training | Card declares CDLA-Sharing-1.0 and says stories were synthetically generated by GPT-3.5/GPT-4. This is not a fit for a strict no-model-derived-data policy unless the owner approves it. | Excluded by default |
| [Project Gutenberg permissions](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission) | Older literary text | Many works are US public domain, but some are copyrighted and non-US users must check local law; trademark/license notices also matter. | Per-book review only |
| [Wikimedia content reuse](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_APIs/Content_reuse) | Encyclopedic text | Most Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0; attribution and ShareAlike apply, with exceptions. | Conditional; legal review |
| [Hugging Face dataset info API](https://huggingface.co/docs/dataset-viewer/en/info) | Verify metadata such as license, homepage, features | Metadata is a starting point, not a legal guarantee; inspect the actual dataset card and files. | Required verification step |

## Initial recommendation

Start with a tiny, manually curated corpus of original Leo-authored examples and specifically reviewed permissively licensed/public-domain text. Add sources gradually. Keep separate train/validation/test splits and do not let near-duplicate documents leak across them.

**Do not automatically ingest Common Crawl, CC-100, FineWeb, OpenWebText, or arbitrary Hub datasets** merely because they are widely used. Their source-content rights and terms need a separate, dataset-specific review.
